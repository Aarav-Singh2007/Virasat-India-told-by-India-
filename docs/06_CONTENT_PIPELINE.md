# 06 - Content Pipeline & Strategy

Building Virasat requires a structured approach to gathering and inputting cultural data.

## 1. Data Sourcing
*   **Primary Focus:** Rajasthan, Kerala, Nagaland, Bihar.
*   **Sources:** Government cultural portals, historical archives, GI tag registries, and direct outreach (if possible) to artisans for "Live Guru Connect" audio snippets.

## 2. Asset Preparation
*   **Audio:** For the hackathon demo, source royalty-free ambient music (e.g., folk instruments) and record realistic voiceovers simulating the "Live Guru Connect" (or use high-quality AI voices strictly as placeholders, making sure to pitch the *concept* of real voices).
*   **Images:** High-resolution, authentic photography of crafts, festivals, and people. Avoid generic stock photos of monuments.

## 3. Heritage Genome Mapping
*   Data entry requires manually mapping the relationships.
*   *Example Chain (Rajasthan):* 
    *   Node A (Jharokha Window)
    *   Node B (Miniature Painting)
    *   Node C (Block Print Textile)
    *   Node D (Living Artisan in Bagru)
    *   *Links:* A -> B, B -> C, C -> D.
