# 01 - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Vision
**Virasat — "India, told by India"**
A cultural discovery engine that goes beyond monument-scanning to showcase the living tapestry of India's heritage. Virasat is a platform to explore India through its languages, crafts, arts, festivals, and people.

## 2. Target Audience
*   **Cultural Tourists & Explorers:** People looking for authentic experiences beyond typical tourist traps.
*   **Students & Researchers:** Individuals studying Indian history, art, and sociology.
*   **Diaspora & Locals:** Users looking to connect with their own roots and discover the living traditions of their home states.

## 3. Core Features
*   **Themed Discovery Map:** An interactive, cinematic map where users travel by train, boat, or helicopter to explore different states.
*   **The Heritage Genome:** A visual, connected web showing the influence chain of culture (e.g., Architecture → Motif → Textile → Artisan).
*   **Live Guru Connect:** Real artisans, priests, and locals narrating their own culture via audio/video, rather than AI text-to-speech.
*   **Personalization Engine:** Surfaces traditions tied to the user's state or surname upon onboarding.
*   **Live "Present-Tense" Layer:** A real-time calendar of festivals and cultural events happening across India today.

## 4. Key User Flows
1.  **Onboarding:** User enters their name/surname and home state. The app personalizes the initial map view.
2.  **Map Exploration:** User selects a transport mode (e.g., train) and clicks on a state (e.g., Rajasthan).
3.  **State Deep Dive:** User is presented with the current live events, history, and the Heritage Genome for that state.
4.  **Genome Interaction:** User clicks through the Heritage Genome nodes, eventually landing on a Live Guru Connect profile or marketplace link.

## 5. Success Metrics for Hackathon
*   **Wow Factor:** Smooth, cinematic map animations.
*   **Depth Demonstration:** Fully fleshed-out Heritage Genome chains for 4 focus states (Rajasthan, Kerala, Nagaland, Bihar).
*   **Differentiation:** Clear visual and functional contrast from standard "monument viewer" apps.
